# Names and terms in the Russian draft

All of these appear in the chapter 1–29 drafts (fuzzy). **Fixed** names were chosen by the user. **Prov.** names were
chosen by Claude before drafting and are in `glossary.tsv`. **New** names were coined by the chapter translators.
To change a name everywhere, edit the PO files (and `glossary.tsv` for enforced ones). `tools/check.py` flags any
place that doesn't match the glossary.
